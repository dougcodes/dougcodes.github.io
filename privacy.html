Privacy Policy

Diamond Hands Alerts · Last updated August 28, 2026

Diamond Hands Alerts ("the Program", "we") is a personal stock-portfolio notification program. It sends automated portfolio alert messages (SMS text messages, voice calls, and email) about the program operator's own investment positions to the program operator's own verified phone number and email address. This policy describes what information the Program collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

The Program collects and stores: (1) the contact information entered into the Program's configuration — a mobile phone number and an email address — and (2) investment position data (stock symbols, prices, and alert thresholds) entered by the program operator. The Program does not collect information from message recipients beyond the phone number they provide when opting in, and it does not use cookies, tracking, or analytics.

How we use information

Phone numbers and email addresses are used for exactly one purpose: delivering portfolio alert notifications from Diamond Hands Alerts to the person who opted in. Position data is used only to determine when an alert should be sent. We send no marketing or promotional content of any kind.

Mobile information and text messaging consent

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with, sold to, or bought by any third parties or affiliates under any circumstances.

Mobile information is transmitted only to our telecommunications service providers (such as Twilio) and email service providers strictly as required to deliver the messages themselves. Those providers act solely on our instructions to deliver messages and are not permitted to use the information for any other purpose.

Text messaging disclosures

Message frequency varies with market activity (typically 0–10 messages per market day). Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out at any time; after texting STOP, no further messages will be sent to your number. Full program terms: Terms and Conditions.

Data retention and security

Data resides on the operator's private, access-controlled systems. Contact information is retained only while alert delivery is active and is deleted on opt-out or on request.

Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reply HELP to any program message, or contact the program operator via GitHub: github.com/dougcodes.
