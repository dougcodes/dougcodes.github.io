Privacy Policy

Diamond Hands Alerts · Last updated August 27, 2026

Diamond Hands Alerts ("the Program") is a personal, single-user notification service. It is a private stock-portfolio monitoring application built, operated, and used by its sole owner. The Program sends portfolio alert messages (SMS, voice calls, and email) exclusively to the owner's own verified phone number and email address. There are no other recipients, subscribers, customers, or users.

Information collected

The Program stores the owner's own contact information (phone number and email address) and the owner's own investment position data (symbols, prices, and alert thresholds). All data is entered by the owner, about the owner, and is stored on the owner's private infrastructure. The Program does not collect any information from or about any other person.

How information is used

Contact information is used for one purpose only: delivering portfolio alert notifications to the owner. Position data is used only to determine when an alert should be sent.

No sharing with third parties

No personal information — including phone numbers, text messaging originator opt-in data, and consent information — is sold, rented, shared with, or disclosed to any third party for any purpose, including marketing or lead generation. Data is transmitted only to the telecommunications and email providers (such as Twilio) strictly as required to deliver the messages themselves.

Marketing

The Program sends no marketing or promotional content of any kind, to anyone.

Data retention and security

Data resides on the owner's private, access-controlled systems and is retained only as long as the owner finds it useful. The owner may delete any or all of it at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to the operator via GitHub: github.com/dougcodes.
