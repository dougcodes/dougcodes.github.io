Program description

Diamond Hands Alerts is a personal stock-portfolio notification program. It sends automated, informational alerts about the program operator's own investment positions — price alerts and stop/target notifications — by SMS text message, voice call, and email. Messages are sent by Diamond Hands Alerts to individuals who have opted in by entering their mobile phone number into the Program's configuration; in practice the sole recipient is the program operator. The Program sends no marketing or promotional content.

Consent and opt-in

By entering a mobile phone number into the Program's configuration, the recipient consents to receive automated portfolio alert text messages and voice calls from Diamond Hands Alerts at that number. Consent is given directly to Diamond Hands Alerts, is specific to this program, and is not a condition of any purchase. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with, sold to, or bought by any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with market activity. On a typical active market day the Program sends between zero and roughly ten messages; on quiet days it sends none.

Fees

Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient's mobile carrier plan. The Program itself charges no fees.

Opt out

Reply STOP to cancel and stop receiving messages at any time. After texting STOP, a final confirmation message may be sent and no further messages will be sent to that number.
