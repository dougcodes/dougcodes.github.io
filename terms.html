Program description

Diamond Hands Alerts is a personal, single-user stock portfolio notification program operated by Douglas Alves. It sends automated alerts about the operator's own investment positions — for example price alerts and stop/target notifications — by SMS text message, voice call, and email, exclusively to the operator's own verified phone number and email address. The program has no subscribers or recipients other than its operator, and it sends no marketing content.

Consent and eligibility

The sole recipient is the program's operator, who configured his own phone number in his own application; that configuration constitutes consent. Messages are sent only to United States numbers. Consent is not shared with or transferred to any third party.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with market activity. On a typical active market day the program sends between zero and roughly ten messages; on quiet days it sends none.

Fees

Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient's mobile carrier plan. The program itself charges no fees.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to cancel and stop receiving messages at any time. After sending STOP, no further messages will be sent to that number. Reply HELP for help, or contact douglas@alvescorp.com.
